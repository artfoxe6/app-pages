PRIVACY POLICY

MinSec Clock Privacy Policy

Effective: July 24, 2026 · Updated: August 9, 2026

MinSec Clock respects and protects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information or usage data with third parties.

1. Data Processing

  • The app reads the system time locally only to display the clock.
  • Time on the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen is presented locally by Apple's Live Activities system feature.
  • Preferences such as clock face, time format, and onboarding state are stored only on the device and are not sent to the developer or third parties.
  • The app contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs.
  • The app does not connect to a developer-operated server, so there is no server-side user data to retain or delete. Ending a Live Activity removes its display state; deleting the app removes locally stored preferences.

2. Third-Party Services

The app does not send user data to third-party services. Apple may process data related to the App Store, operating system, and Live Activities under its own policies.

3. Policy Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and consent will be requested when required.

4. Contact Us

For questions about privacy or using the app, email mckejy@gmail.com or visit the Support page.
