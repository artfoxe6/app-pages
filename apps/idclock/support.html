MinSec Clock Support

Version: 1.0.1 · Updated: August 9, 2026

MinSec Clock runs locally on iPhone and provides a full-screen clock, Lock Screen Live Activities, and Dynamic Island presentation on supported devices.

Why can't I start a Live Activity?

Confirm in Settings that MinSec Clock is allowed to use Live Activities. Dynamic Island presentation requires a supported iPhone; other compatible devices show the Live Activity on the Lock Screen.

Why does a Live Activity stop automatically?

A Live Activity can run for up to eight hours. On iOS 16 and iOS 17, it also stops when crossing midnight to prevent incorrect time presentation on those older systems.

How do I delete saved settings?

MinSec Clock stores preferences such as clock face and time format only on your device. Deleting the app removes those settings. There is no server-side account or data.
